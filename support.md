# Luma Isles — Support

**How to play:** tap a prism to turn it 90°. The light follows live. Guide every beam to the crystal core of its color; blue and red light together make gold. Each level has a par, the fewest taps that solve it; match it for three stars.

**New pieces:** glass diamonds split the light left and right, colored orbs repaint it, switch crystals open the gates, and prisms on rails slide one stop per tap.

**Stuck?** Tap *Hint* to see which piece to turn next. Each level gives free hints, and you can watch an optional ad for more. Retry is always free.

**Map:** levels unlock one by one across four floating isles. Each solve brings its island back to life.

**Ads:** the game is free. An ad may appear between levels (never in the first three), and you can watch an optional video ad for an extra hint.

**Progress:** stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

**Ads and privacy:** see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
